2. What HookNote Does

HookNote performs only two functions:

  1. Record audio and send it to user-configured webhooks.
  2. Send text written by the user to those webhooks.

The app is free, ad-free, and does not include analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. Data Processed

HookNote processes only the minimum data required to function:

  • Content you provide: audio or text.
  • Configured webhooks: alias and URL (1 to 5).
  • Local metadata: delivery status, date/time.

📌 No data is collected, stored, or processed on the Developer’s servers.

4. Purpose & Legal Basis (GDPR)

  • Purpose: to enable sending audio or text to user-configured webhooks.
  • Legal basis:
    • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): providing the requested service.
    • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): microphone use and sending to selected webhooks.

5. Where & How Data Is Processed

  • Processing occurs only on your device.
  • Data is transmitted directly to the configured webhooks without passing through the Developer’s servers.
  • HookNote does not receive, store, or intermediate this data.

6. Retention

  • HookNote does not retain personal data.
  • After sending, the app clears the information.
  • Temporary files (such as recorded audio) may remain on your device and you can delete them.
  • Retention within webhooks depends on the destination service.

For operation, HookNote may request:

  • 📁 Storage: to create, read, or delete temporary files (audio or text).

You can manage or revoke these permissions in your device settings. Missing permissions may prevent some features (e.g., without microphone access, audio cannot be recorded).

9. Security
